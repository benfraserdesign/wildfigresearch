Community co-design with The Green Estate CIC

1 June 2026

Capacity building

Project: Our shared story: learnings from the Open Manor project

In partnership with The Green Estate CIC

We worked with The Green Estate CIC to bring together a community co-design team of twelve residents from Manor Castle Ward, Sheffield, who explored what place-based urban resilience means in practice from a community-led perspective.

Together we collaborated with local people and partners, academic researchers and expert practitioners to share stories and skills and shape a Greenprint for a more connected, nature-rich, and resilient local future.

This report tells the story of our work together through the Open Manor Project, a participatory co-design initiative commissioned by The Green Estate Community Interest Company (CIC) and funded by the South Yorkshire Mayoral Combined Authority and Yorkshire Policy Innovation Partnership.

Open Manor Greenprint sets out what The Green Estate, local partners, and residents might build together: not as a fixed plan imposed from outside, but as a living document that can grow and adapt alongside the community it serves. This is not a plan handed down from outside. It is something planted here, in Manor Castle Ward, by the people who know it best, and grown together, in the hope that it will still be flowering long after our project has ended.

What The Green Estate CIC said

The Green Estate CIC has had the pleasure of commissioning Al Mathers to work collaboratively with us on a community co-design project which ran from July 2025 – July 2026. The project came at a pivotal time for our organisation as we explore what it means to be an urban resilience demonstrator and importantly, what that means for local people.

Al curated a beautiful narrative for the project which followed the seasons and centred around storytelling from the past, present and future. The effort in the detail was outstanding and it was a joy to work with Al from start to finish which included prep work, the launch at our annual Marvellous Meadows event, shortlisting participants, a series of workshops with 12 local people, testing out community meals, the final showcase event and everything in between!

The project would not have been as successful as it was without Al's experience, knowledge and care and we are deeply grateful for that and are excited to continue working with Al as we take forward the outcomes of the project.

Alice Jones

Head of Green & Resilient Communities
