<p>They produced a high quality report based on four case study fieldwork areas, within the deadline. They were able to achieve this thanks to their excellent networks and deep knowledge of the sector, and also their ability to grasp the brief quickly. Drafts were of a very good quality and Al responded to our feedback and comments thoughtfully and helpfully.</p>

<p>As importantly, they were a pleasure to work with. Regular meetings with Al were always informative and enjoyable. It was also very reassuring to have Al present at our related event, where she facilitated a breakout session.
