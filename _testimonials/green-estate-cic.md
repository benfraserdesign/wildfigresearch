<p>The Green Estate CIC has had the pleasure of commissioning Al Mathers to work collaboratively with us on a community co-design project which ran from July 2025 – July 2026. The project came at a pivotal time for our organisation as we explore what it means to be an urban resilience demonstrator and importantly, what that means for local people.</p>

<p>Al curated a beautiful narrative for the project which followed the seasons and centred around storytelling from the past, present and future. The effort in the detail was outstanding and it was a joy to work with Al from start to finish which included prep work, the launch at our annual Marvellous Meadows event, shortlisting participants, a series of workshops with 12 local people, testing out community meals, the final showcase event and everything in between!</p>

<p>The project would not have been as successful as it was without Al's experience, knowledge and care and we are deeply grateful for that and are excited to continue working with Al as we take forward the outcomes of the project.</p>
