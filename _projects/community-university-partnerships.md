<p class="lead">This research, carried out with <a href="https://localtrust.org.uk/" target="_blank">Local Trust</a>, explores what it takes to build genuine, lasting relationships between universities and communities, and what gets in the way.</p>

<p>Drawing on four in-depth case studies from across the UK, we examined partnerships between universities, community organisations, schools and public bodies that have developed over time.</p>

<p>Our report shares stories and practical learning for communities, universities, funders, and policymakers working to build stronger partnerships for place-based change, and highlights the conditions needed to make this work sustainable, equitable and impactful.</p>

<p>This research informed Local Trust's national learning event in June 2026, which brought together universities, community organisations, funders, and policy stakeholders to share good practice and explore what makes community–university partnerships work.</p>
